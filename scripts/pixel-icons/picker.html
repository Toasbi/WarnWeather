Connecting

Every size of every status icon in the four ways the watch can draw it, rendered by the Pebble Time 2 emulator. Tap the version you like for each size, and mark Redraw where you want to draw the pixels again. Pixel is your drawing; Half slant and Slant smooth its steps into slanted lines; Old is the icon from before the pixel drawings, scaled the old way. Pebble 2 and Pebble 2 Duo have no grey pixels, so they always show Pixel.
