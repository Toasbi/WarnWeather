# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Weather for your weather locations from Open-Meteo, worldwide and without a key: now, every hour
  for four days and every day for a week, in your units.
- Hourly rain chance and amount, wind, gusts, UV, pressure, cloud cover, humidity and dew point
  besides the standard weather readings.
- Can be the watch's weather provider (Settings > Weather).
