# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Weather from the Deutscher Wetterdienst for your weather locations in Germany and the countries
  around it, without a key: now, every hour for four days and every day for a week, in your units.
- Hourly rain chance and amount, wind, gusts, pressure, cloud cover, humidity and dew point besides
  the standard weather readings.
- Can be the watch's weather provider (Settings > Weather).
